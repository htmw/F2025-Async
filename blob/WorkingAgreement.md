# Team Working Agreement

- **Team Syncs**: The team meets twice weekly on Tuesdays at 6:00 PM EST to align on progress, barriers, and next steps. Additional meetings may be scheduled as needed.


- **Pair Programming**: Development work is completed in pairs to encourage collaboration, shared understanding, and consistent quality.


- **Ownership & Accountability**: If one partner is unavailable, the remaining partner is expected to continue progress, document updates, and flag risks early.


- **Pair Flexibility**: Pairings may rotate per story or per sprint based on availability, workload, or required expertise to ensure balanced contribution.


- **Story Readiness**: All stories must be groomed, clearly defined, and estimated before entering a sprint.


- **Story Kickoff**: Each story requires a brief kickoff discussion (including 1–2 additional team members when appropriate) to confirm scope, acceptance criteria, and dependencies.


- **Communication Standards**: All updates, blockers, and decisions should be communicated promptly through the agreed team channels.


- **Quality Expectations**: Code must meet agreed standards, include basic testing where applicable, and be reviewed before completion.


- **Respect & Collaboration**: Team members are expected to communicate respectfully, support one another, and maintain a solution-oriented mindset.
